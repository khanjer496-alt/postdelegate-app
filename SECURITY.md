# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security vulnerabilities in PostDelegate privately by email to [security@postdelegate.com](mailto:security@postdelegate.com).

Include a clear description, steps to reproduce, a proof of concept where possible, and the impact you expect. We acknowledge reports within 72 hours and coordinate disclosure with you: please do not share details publicly until a fix is available, and avoid accessing or changing data that is not your own while researching.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release is supported.

## Upstream

PostDelegate is based on the open-source [Postiz](https://github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app) project. Issues that affect upstream Postiz itself can also be reported to its maintainers through their own security policy.
