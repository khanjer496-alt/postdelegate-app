2. How we use data

We use account information to authenticate you, manage access and provide support. We process submitted content to store, display and manage it, and to publish it to the platforms you instruct us to, at the times you schedule. We use connected-account data to show your channels and, where the platform permits, post performance and analytics. We use billing data to start, renew, cancel and invoice subscriptions and prevent fraud, affiliate data to attribute referrals and pay commissions, and technical data to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service. We do not sell personal data, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for advertising.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on contract for providing the service, legitimate interests for proportionate security, reliability, fraud-prevention and affiliate-attribution work, legal obligations such as tax and accounting records, and consent where requested (for example for storing the referral cookie and campaign source in your browser, or for publishing a review).

We do not sell data we receive from platforms, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train generalized AI models.

What we store and why. For each connected account we store the account identifier, display name, picture, username, access and refresh tokens (or API key), token expiry and connection status in our application database, so that we can publish what you schedule and display your analytics. Social-account access and refresh tokens are encrypted in the database with AES-256-GCM, using a dedicated key kept outside the database. User-entered connection credentials (for example app passwords), workspace OpenAI keys and other stored third-party API keys are encrypted in the database with AES-256, using a key derived from a server secret kept outside the database. The database is not exposed on a public port, and off-site backups are encrypted. To show trends, we keep a daily analytics history: daily account counts (followers, following and posts) and each post's latest metrics, title excerpt and link are kept for up to 400 days, and daily per-post metrics for 90 days. YouTube analytics are not stored, apart from a short-lived cache of up to one hour. The analytics history of a channel you delete is removed at the next daily clean-up. The content, schedule and publishing result of posts you create is stored as described in section 13.

How to revoke. Disconnect or delete the channel in PostDelegate (this stops our use of the connection), and also revoke PostDelegate in the platform's own connected-apps settings: Facebook business integrations and Instagram "Apps and websites", Threads and TikTok "Apps and services" in the account's security or privacy settings, LinkedIn permitted services, X connected apps, Pinterest account settings under apps, Google security settings, and the equivalent section of any other platform. Deleting PostDelegate records does not delete content already published on a platform; remove it there. See also our data deletion instructions.

4. Google: sign-in, YouTube and Google Business Profile

Google sign-in requests access to your basic profile and email address. We use your Google account identifier and email address to identify or create your PostDelegate account. Google authorization credentials are exchanged during sign-in so we can obtain that information; PostDelegate then issues its own application session.

These sign-in permissions do not authorize access to your Gmail messages, Drive files, Calendar events or YouTube channel content, and do not authorize posting to a social channel. Any publishing connection requires its own permissions and configuration.

PostDelegate uses YouTube API Services when you separately connect a YouTube channel. With your authorization, we access your Google profile and email address, channel identifiers, names and images, video identifiers and statistics, and channel analytics. We use this information to identify your channel, upload the videos and optional thumbnails you instruct us to publish, record publishing results and display performance.

When you separately connect a Google Business Profile, we access your Google profile and email address and, through the Business Profile APIs, the accounts and locations you manage, their names and media. We use this information to let you choose a location and to publish the posts you instruct us to publish to it.

We store OAuth access and refresh tokens to maintain your YouTube or Google Business Profile connection, together with channel or location details and publishing records in our application database. YouTube channel and video analytics reports are fetched on demand. Earlier server-side analytics cache entries may remain for up to one hour. Publishing sends your selected video, title, description, optional thumbnail, post content, media and publishing settings to Google/YouTube. Connected-channel information and reports are available to authorized workspace members according to their access permissions.

We use Google user data, including YouTube and Google Business Profile data, only for the sign-in and separately authorized features described here, account access, support and security. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising or use it to train generalized AI models, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security purposes, to comply with law, or as needed to provide support you request. PostDelegate's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google's own processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy. By using PostDelegate's YouTube features you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

You can revoke PostDelegate's access through Google's security settings. You can also request deletion of data held by PostDelegate, or contact us about our privacy practices, by emailing support@postdelegate.com, as described in Retention and deletion below. Deleting PostDelegate's records does not delete videos or other data held by YouTube or Google; manage that data through YouTube, YouTube Studio or your Business Profile.

10. AI features and your own assistant

PostDelegate has optional AI features, including AI writing help and the Agent, AI images, automatic captions in Studio (speech transcription with OpenAI's Whisper model) and the script, voiceover and images of Studio's AI UGC videos. They run only on your workspace's own OpenAI API key, which a workspace admin adds in the application. PostDelegate does not provide or pay for AI: OpenAI bills you directly for what your key uses. Without a key, the AI features are switched off and nothing is sent to OpenAI.

When your workspace has added a key and you use an AI feature, we send OpenAI, under your key, the prompts, text, audio and images needed for that feature: for example your prompt or Agent chat messages and the post and channel details the request involves, the audio of a Studio clip you ask us to caption, or a script you ask us to write or voice. OpenAI processes this data under your OpenAI account and its own terms and privacy policy. We store the key encrypted, use it only for your workspace's AI requests and delete it when you remove it. We do not sell this data or use it to train AI models.

Separately, you can choose to connect your own AI assistant (for example Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor) to PostDelegate through MCP and OAuth. If you do, the assistant acts with the access you authorize, and the data it reads or sends is processed by that provider under its own terms and privacy policy. You can revoke the connection in PostDelegate or with the provider.

12. Cookies, browser storage and analytics

We use the cookies and browser storage the service needs to work. Anything else is optional and used only if you agree in our cookie banner. We do not currently use advertising cookies or analytics.

Cookie banner. On your first visit to postdelegate.com, or to the sign-in and sign-up pages of app.postdelegate.com, a banner asks for your choice. It offers three equal options: Reject non-essential, Customize and Accept all. Customize shows each category with its own switch:

  • Strictly necessary (always on): signing in, security, your settings and the choice itself.
  • Referral and campaign attribution (off unless you agree): the pd_ref cookie and the saved campaign source described below.
  • Analytics: shown only if we switch on an analytics tool. None is switched on today, so the banner does not show this category.

Until you agree to a category, nothing in it is stored on your device and no analytics script is loaded. If you reject, the referral code and campaign values stay only in the links and addresses of that visit: if you sign up with your email address during that visit, the code is sent with the sign-up form and the account can still be attributed to the referrer. Your choices are kept in the pd_consent cookie for 6 months, after which we ask again. You can change them at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer of postdelegate.com, on the sign-in pages and in the app's settings. Switching a category off removes what it stored: on the site straight away, and in the app (the referral cookie and the saved campaign source) the next time you open it, before you can sign up. A referral cookie saved by an earlier version of the site, before the banner existed, can still be used until it expires (at most 60 days), and a campaign source saved then stays in your browser and can still be sent at checkout, unless you reject the category or clear your browser storage.

13. Retention and deletion

  • Account data and content: kept while your account or workspace exists.
  • Deleted accounts and workspaces: when you delete your account, it and any workspace that only you belong to are removed from active use straight away, and their connected channels stop being used. You cannot delete your account while you are the only owner of a workspace that other people use: first make another member the owner in Settings → Team, or remove the other members. Workspaces that have another owner stay with their members. To have the remaining records, connected-channel credentials and uploaded media permanently erased, send a deletion request (see Data deletion); we complete verified requests within 30 days.
  • Disconnected channels: we stop using the credentials for a channel you disconnect or delete, and remove them from our systems as part of deletion requests.
  • Backups: backup copies are not restored into active use. Encrypted off-site backups are kept on a rotation of 14 daily and 8 weekly copies, so copies of deleted data expire from them within about 8 weeks. Backup copies also kept on the application server, which only administrators can access, do not yet expire automatically and are removed by an administrator.
  • Logs: operational and security logs are rotated automatically and are not kept as a long-term archive: application and web-server logs are capped in size and are replaced when the service is updated, and operating-system logs are rotated weekly. Logs may be kept longer when needed to investigate abuse or a security incident.
  • Billing and affiliate records: invoices, Stripe identifiers and the affiliate commission ledger may be kept after deletion as long as needed for tax, accounting and legal obligations, and as a record of commissions owed or paid.
  • Reviews: kept until you ask us to delete or withdraw them; withdrawn reviews are no longer displayed.
  • Uploaded media: deleting a record may not immediately remove the stored object or cached copies; see section 11.

To request access, correction, export or deletion, email support@postdelegate.com; see also Data Deletion Instructions. We may need to verify your identity and clarify which account or workspace the request concerns. Legal requirements, security needs, recovery copies and the rights of other people or workspace members may affect what can be removed and when. We will explain applicable retention and report the request's completion status. A deletion request does not automatically erase every cached or recovery copy before it expires.

14. Security

The public application and media host use HTTPS. The application's web server refuses requests that do not arrive through Cloudflare, application databases and supporting services are not exposed through public database ports, local account passwords are hashed, and access to production systems is restricted. Off-site backups are encrypted before they leave the server. Connected-account tokens, user-entered connection credentials and stored API keys are encrypted at rest, as described in section 3. No system is perfectly secure. Report suspected vulnerabilities to security@postdelegate.com.
