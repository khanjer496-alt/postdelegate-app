Data Processing Addendum

PostDelegate — Nasida Apps LLC
Last updated: 2 November 2026 · Version 1.0

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service (the "Agreement") between Nasida Apps LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company that operates PostDelegate ("PostDelegate", "we", "us", the "Processor"), and the customer that has agreed to the Agreement ("Customer", "you", the "Controller"). It applies whenever PostDelegate processes Customer Personal Data on your behalf in providing the service. It takes effect automatically when you accept the Agreement; no signature is required. If you need a countersigned copy, email support@postdelegate.com.

If this DPA conflicts with the Agreement, this DPA prevails for the processing of Customer Personal Data. If the Standard Contractual Clauses (section 11) apply and conflict with this DPA, the Standard Contractual Clauses prevail.

1. Definitions

  • "Data Protection Laws" means all laws that apply to the processing of Customer Personal Data under the Agreement, including Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the "GDPR"), the GDPR as it forms part of UK law and the UK Data Protection Act 2018 (the "UK GDPR"), the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act and its regulations (the "CCPA").
  • "Customer Personal Data" means personal data that you or your users submit to the service, or that PostDelegate collects from platforms you connect, and that PostDelegate processes on your behalf as a processor or service provider.
  • "Sub-processor" means a third party engaged by PostDelegate that processes Customer Personal Data on PostDelegate's behalf.
  • "Security Incident" means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data.
  • "Standard Contractual Clauses" or "SCCs" means the clauses annexed to Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021.
  • "UK Addendum" means the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU SCCs issued by the UK Information Commissioner under s119A of the Data Protection Act 2018 (version B1.0, in force 21 March 2022), as amended.
  • "controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data", "processing", "service provider", "business", "sell" and "share" have the meanings given in the applicable Data Protection Laws.

2. Roles and scope

2.1 Customer as controller. For Customer Personal Data, you are the controller (or, where you act for your own client, a processor, in which case PostDelegate is your sub-processor). PostDelegate is your processor and, under the CCPA, your service provider.

2.2 PostDelegate as controller for its own data. PostDelegate acts as an independent controller, under its Privacy Policy, for personal data it processes for its own business purposes: account administration and authentication, billing and subscriptions (with Stripe), affiliate attribution and payouts, fraud prevention, security logging, support correspondence, and compliance with legal obligations. This DPA does not apply to that processing.

2.3 Social and publishing platforms are your destinations, not our Sub-processors. When you connect a social or publishing account (for example Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, Google Business Profile, X, Pinterest, Reddit, Discord, Slack, Telegram, Mastodon, Bluesky, WordPress, Medium, Dev.to, Hashnode or Listmonk) and instruct PostDelegate to publish to it or read analytics from it, that platform receives and processes data under its own terms and privacy policy, as a recipient you choose. Those platforms are not engaged by PostDelegate and are not PostDelegate's Sub-processors. The same applies to:

  • OpenAI, when a workspace admin adds the workspace's own OpenAI API key: requests are sent under your key and your OpenAI account; and
  • AI assistants you connect to PostDelegate through MCP or OAuth (for example Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor).

2.4 Details of processing. The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, and the types of personal data and categories of data subjects, are set out in Annex I.

3. Your instructions

3.1 PostDelegate will process Customer Personal Data only on your documented instructions, unless required to do otherwise by law to which PostDelegate is subject. In that case PostDelegate will tell you about that legal requirement before processing, unless the law prohibits it on important grounds of public interest.

3.2 The Agreement, this DPA, your configuration and use of the service (for example the channels you connect, the posts you schedule and the settings you choose) and any other written instructions you give and PostDelegate accepts are your complete documented instructions.

3.3 PostDelegate will tell you promptly if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws. PostDelegate may suspend the affected processing until you confirm or change the instruction.

3.4 You are responsible for the lawfulness of the Customer Personal Data you submit and of your instructions, including having a legal basis and giving any notices needed for PostDelegate to process the data and to publish content to the platforms you choose.

4. Confidentiality

PostDelegate ensures that every person it authorizes to process Customer Personal Data is bound by a duty of confidentiality (contractual or statutory) and accesses the data only as needed to provide, secure or support the service.

5. Security

5.1 PostDelegate implements and maintains appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Customer Personal Data, taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context and purposes of the processing and the risks to data subjects, as required by Article 32 GDPR / UK GDPR. The current measures are described in Annex II.

5.2 PostDelegate may update these measures over time, provided the overall level of protection is not reduced.

5.3 You are responsible for the security of your own accounts and devices, including keeping your login credentials and API keys confidential, choosing who you invite to your workspace, and not uploading media that must stay private (uploaded media is served from public URLs; see Privacy Policy section 11).

6. Sub-processors

6.1 General authorization. You give PostDelegate general written authorization to engage Sub-processors. The Sub-processors in use on the date above are listed in Annex III and on our Sub-processors page, and you authorize them.

6.2 Sub-processor terms. PostDelegate will engage each Sub-processor under a written contract that imposes data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA, to the extent applicable to the service the Sub-processor provides. PostDelegate remains responsible to you for each Sub-processor's performance of its obligations.

6.3 Notice of changes. PostDelegate will give at least 30 days' notice before adding or replacing a Sub-processor, by updating Annex III (or the sub-processor page linked from it) and by email to the workspace owner's address if you subscribe to updates by writing to support@postdelegate.com with the subject "Sub-processor updates". In an emergency (for example to keep the service secure or available), PostDelegate may give shorter notice and will explain why.

6.4 Objection. You may object to a new Sub-processor on reasonable data protection grounds by emailing support@postdelegate.com within the notice period. The parties will discuss the objection in good faith. If PostDelegate cannot offer a reasonable alternative, you may terminate the affected part of the service before the change takes effect, and PostDelegate will refund any prepaid fees for the unused remainder of the term for that part.

7. Data subject requests

7.1 Taking into account the nature of the processing, PostDelegate will assist you by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as possible, to respond to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and the CCPA rights to know, delete and correct).

7.2 Much of this is self-service: you can view, edit and delete posts, media records, channels and workspace members in the application, and disconnect connected accounts at any time.

7.3 If PostDelegate receives a request directly from a data subject that identifies you as the controller, it will not respond except to direct the person to you (unless required by law), and will forward the request to you without undue delay.

8. Security Incidents

8.1 PostDelegate will notify you without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident affecting Customer Personal Data. Notice goes to the workspace owner's email address.

8.2 The notice will describe, as far as then known: the nature of the incident, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed to address it and mitigate its effects, and a contact point. Where information is not yet available, PostDelegate will provide it in phases without undue further delay.

8.3 PostDelegate will take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remedy the incident, and will provide reasonable assistance so you can meet your own obligations to notify supervisory authorities and data subjects. Notifying you is not an admission of fault or liability.

9. Assistance with assessments

Taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to it, PostDelegate will provide reasonable assistance with your data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with supervisory authorities, under Articles 32 to 36 GDPR / UK GDPR, to the extent you cannot reasonably meet them with the information in this DPA, the Privacy Policy and the service's documentation.

10. Deletion or return at the end of the service

10.1 When the Agreement ends, or earlier on your written request, PostDelegate will, at your choice, delete or return Customer Personal Data. You can delete your account in the application, which removes it, and any workspace that only you belong to, from active use straight away. To receive a copy of your content, or to have the remaining records, connected-channel credentials and uploaded media permanently erased, email support@postdelegate.com; PostDelegate will complete verified requests within 30 days (see the Data Deletion Instructions).

10.2 Backup copies are not restored into active use. Encrypted off-site backups expire on their rotation (14 daily and 8 weekly copies, so within about 8 weeks). Backup copies also kept on the application server, which only administrators can access, do not yet expire automatically and are removed by an administrator. Operational logs are rotated automatically and are not kept as a long-term archive (see Annex II, item 8). Deleting a media record in the application does not by itself remove the stored file; stored files are removed when a deletion request is completed, and copies served through public URLs may remain in third-party caches for a short time afterwards.

10.3 PostDelegate may keep Customer Personal Data where the law requires it to. It will keep that data confidential and process it only for the purpose the law requires.

11.5 Alternative mechanisms. If PostDelegate or a Sub-processor adopts another valid transfer mechanism (for example certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension), that mechanism may be relied on instead, to the extent permitted.

Annex II — Technical and organizational security measures

  1. Encryption in transit. All public traffic to postdelegate.com, app.postdelegate.com and media.postdelegate.com uses HTTPS/TLS, terminated at Cloudflare's edge and re-encrypted to the origin. The application's web server refuses requests that do not arrive through Cloudflare.
  2. Encryption at rest for secrets. Social-account access and refresh tokens are encrypted in the database with AES-256-GCM, using a dedicated key kept outside the database. User-entered connection credentials (for example app passwords), workspace OpenAI keys and other stored third-party API keys are encrypted in the database with AES-256, using a key derived from a server secret kept outside the database. Server-side secrets are kept in a root-only environment file, not in source code or the repository. Local account passwords are stored only as salted hashes (bcrypt).
  3. Network isolation. Databases, cache and the scheduling service run on the application server and are not exposed on public ports. Administrative access to the server uses key-based SSH only (password and root logins are disabled), with a host firewall and automated blocking of repeated failed SSH logins in place.
  4. Media storage. Uploaded media is stored in Cloudflare R2 object storage and served from media.postdelegate.com over HTTPS. (Media objects are retrievable by URL; see Privacy Policy section 11.)
  5. Backups. Databases and configuration are backed up daily on the application server, where only administrators can access them. A copy is encrypted on the server with age before it leaves it, and stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket that is separate from the media bucket. Off-site rotation: 14 daily and 8 weekly copies. Restore procedures are documented and have been tested.
  6. Access control. Production access is limited to the owner and authorized administrators on a need-to-know basis, with unique accounts and strong, password-manager-generated credentials and multi-factor authentication on provider consoles where available. Within the application, workspace roles control what each member can see and do; administrator "impersonation" for support is restricted to authorized PostDelegate staff.
  7. Least privilege for platforms. PostDelegate requests only the platform permissions needed for the features you use, and uses connected-account tokens only to perform your instructions. Disconnecting a channel stops their use.
  8. Monitoring and availability. Uptime, disk and backup-failure checks run every 10 minutes and alert the administrator. Operational and security logs are rotated automatically and are not kept as a long-term archive: application and web-server logs are capped in size and are replaced when the service is updated, and operating-system logs are rotated weekly; logs may be kept longer when needed to investigate abuse or a Security Incident. A separate staging environment is used to test releases before production.
  9. Payment data. Card data is entered directly with Stripe; PostDelegate does not receive or store full card numbers.
  10. Incident response. Security Incidents are contained, investigated and remediated, and customers are notified within 72 hours (section 8).
  11. Vulnerability management. Operating system security updates are applied automatically and dependency updates are applied regularly; vulnerabilities can be reported to security@postdelegate.com.
  12. Sub-processor diligence. Sub-processors are reputable infrastructure providers bound by data processing terms (Annex III).
  13. Data minimization and deletion. Analytics are read from connected platforms, and a daily history is kept so the service can show trends: daily account counts (followers, following and posts) and each post's latest metrics, title excerpt and link are kept for up to 400 days, and daily per-post metrics for 90 days. YouTube analytics are not stored, apart from a short-lived cache of up to one hour. The analytics history of a deleted channel is removed at the next daily clean-up. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days (section 10).
