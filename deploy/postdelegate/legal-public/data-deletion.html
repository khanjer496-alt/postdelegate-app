Data Deletion Instructions

PostDelegate is operated by Nasida Apps LLC. Effective date: 31 October 2026

You can remove a connected social account, delete your PostDelegate account, or ask us to delete data we hold about you. Each option is described below. This page is also the data deletion instructions URL we give to Meta (Facebook, Instagram and Threads) and TikTok.

1. Disconnect a social account

  1. Sign in at app.postdelegate.com.
  2. In the channel list, open the menu on the channel you want to remove and choose to disable or delete it.

PostDelegate then stops using that connection. You can also revoke PostDelegate's access on the platform itself at any time. On Facebook and Instagram, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) and remove PostDelegate; Threads access can be removed from your Threads or Instagram account settings. On TikTok, go to Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services and remove PostDelegate. On LinkedIn, YouTube, Google Business Profile, X, Pinterest and other platforms, use the connected apps section of that platform's security or privacy settings.

2. Delete your PostDelegate account

  1. Sign in and open Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.

This removes your account from active use straight away. Workspaces that only you belong to are removed from active use too, and their connected channels stop being used. Workspaces shared with other people keep their channels; ask the workspace owner to disconnect them. To have your records, channel credentials and media permanently erased, also send the request in section 3.

3. Ask us to delete your data

Email support@postdelegate.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. Send it from the email address on your account, or tell us which account, workspace or connected social profile the request concerns. If you used PostDelegate with Facebook, Instagram, Threads or TikTok, say so and include your profile name or username on that platform so we can find the connection. We may ask you to verify your identity. You do not need an active PostDelegate account to make a request about data we received from Meta or TikTok.

We confirm receipt and complete verified requests within 30 days. We then confirm what was deleted, including any Meta or TikTok platform data, and explain anything we must keep, for example billing and tax records, an affiliate commission ledger, or data needed for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons. Copies in recovery backups are encrypted and expire on our normal backup rotation (within about 8 weeks) and are not restored into active use.

4. Content already published

Deleting PostDelegate data does not delete posts that were already published to a social platform. Remove those directly on that platform.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy and the Meta and TikTok sections of it.
