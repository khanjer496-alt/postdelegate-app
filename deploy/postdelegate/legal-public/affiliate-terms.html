Effective date: 31 October 2026

These terms govern the PostDelegate affiliate program, operated by Nasida Apps LLC ("we", "us"). By sharing your referral link or code, or by providing payout details, you ("affiliate") agree to them together with the Terms of Service.

1. How the program works

  • Every PostDelegate user has a personal referral link and code on the Affiliates page of the application.
  • When someone opens your link, a first-party cookie (pd_ref) is stored in their browser on postdelegate.com and app.postdelegate.com for 60 days. If they register an account within that period, the new account is attributed to you. The first referral link a person uses is the one that counts, and attribution cannot be changed afterwards.
  • You earn 30% of what the referred account pays us, on each paid invoice, during the first 12 months after its first paid invoice. Taxes are not part of the amount the commission is calculated on.
  • Free periods, trials (including the $1 card authorization that is voided at the start of a trial), unpaid, failed, refunded and charged-back payments do not earn commission.
  • Commission is paid on invoices of the referred account's workspace as billed through Stripe; the 12 months run from the referred account's first paid invoice, not from sign-up or the start of the trial.

2. Hold period, approval and payouts

  • A commission is held for 30 days after the customer's payment. After the hold it becomes approved, unless it was reversed.
  • Approved commissions are paid manually, once a month, once your approved balance reaches 50 USD. Smaller balances roll over to the next month.
  • We pay to the PayPal address you provide on the Affiliates page, or by another method we agree with you. You are responsible for keeping the payout details correct. We may delay a payout until we can verify them.
  • Payouts are made in US dollars. Fees charged by your payment provider or bank are yours.

3. Refunds and chargebacks

If a customer is refunded or disputes a payment, the matching commission is reversed in proportion to the refunded or disputed amount. If a reversed commission was already paid to you, we may deduct it from future payouts or ask you to return it.

4. Not allowed

  • Referring yourself, your own organization, or accounts you control, including by creating accounts with variations of your own email address.
  • Spam: unsolicited bulk messages, posting your link in places where it is unwelcome, or misleading people about what PostDelegate is or costs.
  • Bidding on the "PostDelegate" name, its misspellings or close variations as keywords in paid search, or running ads that imitate us or imply you are us.
  • Using cookie stuffing, forced clicks, hidden redirects or any other method that places the referral cookie without a person knowingly choosing to follow your link.
  • Breaking the law or the rules of the platforms where you promote us, including disclosing that you earn a commission where the law or the platform requires it.

Commissions earned through activity that breaks these rules are not payable, and we may reverse them and remove you from the program.

7. Personal data

We store your referral code and, if you provide it, the PayPal payout email address (as plain text), and use them only to pay you and to contact you about the program. This ledger is kept as a financial record even after a referred workspace is deleted. The pd_ref cookie stores only the referral code and a timestamp. Referred customers are shown to you only in anonymized form. PayPal receives your payout email and the amount when we pay you. See the Privacy Policy for more, including your rights over this data.
